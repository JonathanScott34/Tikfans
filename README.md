# About Us
We are a streaming distribution network built for creators.

On traditional platforms, traffic is dictated by algorithms and revenue is taken layer by layer. Complex systems and rules stand between creators and their supporters. We want to change that.

Through decentralized payments, higher revenue shares, and more direct follow relationships, value can flow freely between creators and users.

## What We Do
- Freely publish videos and articles
- Charge audiences directly
- Earn up to 90% of the revenue
- Participate in platform growth through referrals and earn rewards
- Use Web3 wallets for global, permissionless transactions

The platform is not a middleman — it is a tool.

## Why Now
The creator economy is growing rapidly, yet the people who truly benefit remain limited. The next generation of platforms should be more open, transparent, fair, and global.

The technology is ready. Now it's time for products and communities.

## Our Vision
To make creation a capability that can accumulate over time, be truly owned, and continuously generate value.

> Whether you are a creator, an audience member, or a promoter, you deserve your share of the ecosystem.
